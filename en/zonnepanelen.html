Good to know

Solar panels, from roof to bill.

Why glass-glass

A conventional panel has glass on the front and a plastic film on the back. That film ages: it discolours, lets moisture through and loses its insulating power over the years.

In a glass-glass module the cell package sits between two layers of glass. There is no film that can age. That makes the module less sensitive to moisture, ammonia and temperature swings, and it is the reason manufacturers dare to give longer performance warranties on them.

Your roof first, the quote after

We do not give a price based on an aerial photo. We come out, measure the roof, look at orientation, pitch, roof windows, chimneys and shade, and check your electrical installation.

You then get a layout showing where every panel goes, a calculated annual output and a payback time worked out on your own usage.

North-facing or shaded? We say so honestly

A north-facing or partly shaded roof does not rule out solar panels, but it does lower the output. With optimisers or a hybrid inverter the damage stays limited, because a panel in shade then does not hold back the whole string.

If your roof really is unfavourable, we tell you, rather than selling you something that will never pay for itself.
