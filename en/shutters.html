We build with Roma — German engineering, made to last.

Koen fits Roma roller shutters and sun protection for one reason: they are built to outlast the house. German-made, independently verified, and engineered so the shutter you install today still runs quietly twenty years on — with next to nothing to maintain. It is also one of the most sustainable choices on the market.

🌍

World-first environmental proof

Roma was the first manufacturer in the world to earn Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) for complete sun-protection systems, independently verified by IBU. You see exactly what goes into the product.

Passive House certified

Selected Roma systems are certified Passive House components — among the highest standards for thermal insulation and airtightness, certified since 2013.
