Energy management (EMS)

How do I lower my bill without thinking about it every day?

Short answer

By having your installation controlled. Electricity you do not use yourself, you sell for a fraction of what you will later pay to get it back. That is why our own energy-management software decides, every quarter of an hour, where your energy goes: hot-water tank, battery or charge point.

You have nothing to remember and nothing to set. The software comes with your installation, with no subscription.

⚡

More self-consumption

Every kWh you use instead of exporting is straight saving. The software shifts boiler, battery and charger to the hours your panels are producing.

📉

Capacity tariff flattened

Your bill depends partly on your highest quarter-hour peak. Peak shaving absorbs that peak with the battery — you don't pay for one moment when everything ran at once.

💰

Buy when power is cheap

When prices are low or even negative the battery charges; at expensive hours your home lives on what it stored.

🧩

No subscription

Our own software, supplied with your installation. No monthly licence, no third party holding your data or your controls.

In plain language

What controls actually deliver.

Why controls only make the difference now

For as long as your meter ran backwards, nobody had to think about timing. What you generated at midday you simply took back in the evening. The hour at which you switched something on made no difference.

That is over. Today there are two prices: a low one for what you export and a high one for what you draw. The hour at which your hot-water tank or your car starts working is therefore suddenly worth money.

That is precisely what controls do: shift your usage to the right hours. Every day, without you thinking about it.

What “a decision every quarter of an hour” means in your house

Every quarter of an hour the software sees how much your panels are generating, how much your home is using and how full the battery is. On that basis it chooses where your electricity goes.

If there is a surplus, it goes to the hot-water tank, the battery or the car instead of to the grid. If there is too little, your home lives off what it stored earlier. If output falls away, you get a signal rather than a surprise on your annual statement.

Your highest quarter-hour sets part of your bill

Part of your grid charge is calculated not on how much you use, but on your highest quarter-hour peak. If you cook while the tumble dryer is running and the car is charging, that single quarter of an hour sets your monthly peak — even if it happens only once. In 2026 a minimum of 2.5 kW is applied.

Controls flatten that peak by not letting everything come on at once. You therefore no longer have to plan a household around your meter. Read also the capacity tariff explained.

Why we wrote this software ourselves

Because we fit and maintain the installations ourselves. When something goes wrong, we want to see it before the customer rings — and that does not work with third-party software we cannot get into.

It is included with your installation, with no monthly licence. So there is also no third party holding your data or the control of your home.
