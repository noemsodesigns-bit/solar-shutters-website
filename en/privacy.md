# Privacy statement

Last updated 28 July 2026.

This statement explains what happens to the data you send us through this website. It is written to be read, not to put you off.

## What data we receive

We collect only what you fill in yourself on the request form: your name, phone number and email address, whether you own or rent, when you want the work done, your preferred date and time for a site inspection, and any message you add.

Depending on what you are asking about, technical details about your home are added: the roof type and its orientation, your annual usage, what you are interested in and whether you are considering a charge point.

We deliberately do not ask for a national register number, bank details or a copy of your identity card through this website.

## Why we use it

To answer your request, arrange a site inspection and then send you a quote. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to anyone.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending the form, and after that the steps needed to reach an agreement.

## How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to an order are kept for as long as it is reasonable that you might come back to your question: [TO BE CONFIRMED] . If you become a customer, we keep your file for as long as the law requires for invoicing and warranty.

## Who else sees your data

We work with a small number of suppliers that are necessary to run this website:

- The form on this site is handled by Formspree, which passes what you filled in to our mailbox.
- The website itself is hosted on Vercel servers.
- The typefaces are loaded through Google Fonts, which means your IP address reaches Google.

At these suppliers, data may be processed outside the European Union. Beyond that we pass nothing to third parties unless the law obliges us to.

## Cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies and does not measure your behaviour. We only remember, inside your own browser, which language you chose, so you do not have to choose again on every visit. That choice never leaves your device.

## What you can ask for

You have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict its use or object to it, and to receive it in a readable file. An email to koen.dewolf@alu-dewolf.be is enough; we reply within a month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle that, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels.

## Changes

If anything changes in how we handle your data, we update this page and change the date at the top.
