## Why the inverter decides everything.

## What an inverter actually does

Your panels make a kind of electricity that no appliance in your house can use. The inverter converts it into the electricity that comes out of your socket. Everything your roof generates therefore passes through it.

Think of it as the engine of a car: the panels are the fuel, but the engine decides what you actually do with it. That is why this is the part where a cheap choice makes itself felt the longest.

## “Hybrid” in plain language

A hybrid inverter can do one extra thing: it can also run a battery. You do not have to buy that battery today. You are only keeping the door open.

If you fit an ordinary inverter now and decide you want storage in three years' time, a second unit gets added to your board. That is money you need not have spent. This is why we always ask during the site inspection whether a battery is ever in your plans — even when the answer is “maybe”.
