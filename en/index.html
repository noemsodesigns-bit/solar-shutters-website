Included with every installation

Your system runs itself — on smart energy-management software.

Since the digital meter, the meter no longer runs backwards: power you don't use yourself is sold for a fraction of what you later pay to buy it back. So your system runs on energy-management software — it decides, every quarter hour, where your energy goes. Included with your installation, no subscription.

⚡

More self-consumption

Every kWh you use instead of exporting is straight saving. The software shifts boiler, battery and charger to the hours your panels are producing.

📉

Capacity tariff flattened

Your bill depends partly on your highest quarter-hour peak. Peak shaving absorbs that peak with the battery — you don't pay for one moment when everything ran at once.

💰

Buy when power is cheap

When prices are low or even negative the battery charges; at expensive hours your home lives on what it stored.

🧩

No subscription

Our software, supplied with your installation. No monthly licence, no third party holding your data or your controls.
